The Problem

Missed calls aren't
an inconvenience.
They're revenue bleeding out.

  • Small businesses miss a large share of inbound calls weekly, especially during jobs and after hours
  • Most callers who hit voicemail never call back. They move to the next Google result
  • After-hours calls are the highest-intent leads: people calling at 9pm want to book, not browse
  • Every missed call is revenue handed to a competitor who simply picked up
The emotional spine
This framing (missed calls = missed money) is the core message behind every piece of content and every yard sign.
