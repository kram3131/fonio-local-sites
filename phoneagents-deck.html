The Problem

Missed calls aren't
an inconvenience.
They're revenue bleeding out.

  • Small businesses miss a large share of inbound calls every week, especially during jobs and after hours
  • Most callers who hit voicemail never call back. They move to the next result on Google
  • After-hours calls are the highest-intent leads: people calling at 9pm want to book, not browse
  • Every missed call is revenue handed directly to a competitor who picked up

Content System

Every piece of content
does one of three jobs.

  • STOP: The hook earns the watch. First 1.5 seconds either stop the scroll or lose the viewer. Every Reel opens with a missed call, a dollar figure, or a live AI moment.
  • SHARE: The body is built for the DM forward. "Watch this, it's your business." Business owners who see this think of someone specific. That's the share trigger.
  • CONVERT: The CTA routes to comment "DEMO." Automation delivers the landing page link. Form capture flows into GHL and kicks off a trial.
Posting cadence

3 Reels + 2 Carousels / week

Reels for reach. Carousels for saves and engagement rate. Both formats feeding the same CTA.

Account positioning

Niche over broad

Not an AI commentary account. Purely about small business phone AI. Tight niche grows faster and converts better than general content.
